FAQ

The questions I hear most.

What exactly do you do?

I help companies market and scale digital products — as a marketing & growth consultant, a product/digital sparring partner, through workshops, and by building SEO websites that measurably generate leads.

Why do you build websites if you're a marketing consultant?

Because a website is only worth something if it gets found and generates leads. I build websites from a marketing and SEO perspective — not as pure design. This page is the best proof.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both. I'm based in Cologne and work across the DACH region and remotely — internationally in English. Workshops are delivered on site too.

How does a collaboration start?

With a short, no-obligation call. Afterwards you get a clear assessment of what makes sense — and what doesn't. I usually reply within 48 hours.
