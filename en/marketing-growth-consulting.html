FAQ

In short.

What is a fractional CMO?

An experienced marketing chief on a part-time basis. Instead of filling a full-time role, you bring in strategy, execution power and leadership for a defined period — exactly in the growth phase where it counts.

Which companies is the consulting for?

Mainly startups and scale-ups with product-market fit that want to scale acquisition, as well as mid-sized companies that want to professionalise their online marketing.

Strategic or hands-on?

Both. I develop the strategy and implement it with the team — I come from operational practice, not from a slide deck.
